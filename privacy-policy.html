Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · applies to Vale 2.0 and later

In short

  • Your tasks, routines, focus history and settings are stored only on your device. We have no servers and no accounts.
  • Vale is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some data to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Ads are only personalized if you allow it (Apple's tracking prompt and, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form).
  • There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Who we are

Vale - Focus & Relax ("Vale", "the App") is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App handles and why.

Data stored on your device

Vale keeps the following only on your device, using Apple's standard storage:

  • Tasks and routines: names, colors, icons, focus settings, schedules, reminder times and which days you marked them done
  • Focus history: when you focused, for how long, how many cycles, and which task it was for
  • Templates and preferences: saved session templates, theme, sound and break settings

We never receive this data. It is not uploaded anywhere and is deleted when you delete the App.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders for a task, Vale schedules local notifications on your device. They are created by the App on your iPhone; no notification data is sent to us or to anyone else.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Vale shows ads (banners, a native ad on Today, an occasional full-screen ad after a finished session, and an optional ad you can watch for 5 extra break minutes). Ads never appear during a focus session or a breathing exercise. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, which runs inside the App and may collect:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and other app- or developer-scoped identifiers
  • Coarse location: approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Advertising data and product interactions: which ads were shown and whether they were tapped
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data of the ad SDK

Google uses this data to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking (all regions): after your first finished session, Vale explains why it asks, then iOS asks whether Vale may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized with your advertising identifier and Vale works exactly the same. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: Vale shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can accept, decline, or manage options, and change your choice later in Vale › Settings › Privacy choices.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, health data or precise location.
  • We do not use our own analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell your data. We have no access to the data on your device.

Children

Vale is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping or deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete the App, which removes all of it. Data held by Google is governed by Google's policies; you can manage it in your Google ad settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
