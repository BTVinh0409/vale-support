Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Vale ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Vale - Focus & Relax ("the App").

Information We Collect

Information Stored Locally

Vale stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Session History: Records of your completed focus sessions, including duration and cycles completed
  • User Preferences: Your settings such as theme preference, sound selection, breathing pattern, and timer configurations
  • Saved Presets: Custom focus session presets you create

This data is stored using Apple's standard data storage mechanisms and never leaves your device.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contacts or calendar information
  • Health or fitness data
  • Photos or media
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Any data to external servers

Third-Party Services

Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks. These services may collect certain information as described in their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy

You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking

No Other Third-Party Services

We do not use any analytics, crash reporting, or other third-party services that collect user data.

Data Security

All your data is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's built-in security features, including:

  • Device passcode/Face ID/Touch ID protection
  • iOS data encryption
  • App sandboxing

Data Retention

Your data remains on your device until:

  • You delete the App
  • You clear the App's data in device settings
  • You manually delete your session history within the App

Children's Privacy

Vale does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App is suitable for all ages and rated 4+ on the App Store.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your information:

  • Access: View your data within the App
  • Delete: Remove the App to delete all associated data
  • Portability: Your data stays on your device

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

Summary

  • All data stays on your device
  • We don't collect personal information
  • We don't track your usage
  • Ads may be shown via third-party networks
  • You control your data completely
